RepurposeForge uses your own Google Gemini API key. Google sets the limits and any charges for that key. Check your plan in Google AI Studio.

The key is stored only in your browser’s local storage and sent only to Google’s Gemini API. Local storage is not encrypted, so use the app on a device you trust. You can clear the key at any time.

A computer works best. Phones can open the file in a browser, but it is less convenient.
